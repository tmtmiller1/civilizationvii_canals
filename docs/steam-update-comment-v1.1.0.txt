===== comment 1 of 3 (post first) =====
Canals 1.1.0 is up. Part 1 of 3, new:
• Saves no longer wipe out canals. In 1.0.0 a canal reloaded as open sea, a plain strait with no channel or quays. Saves now store the tile as land and the mod reopens it after the load, drawn as a canal.
• Canals carry over to the next age. They are set to land when the age ends and reopened once the new age has loaded.
• The AI builds canals, on a tile it works that joins two separate bodies of water, paid with city production (or gold when it has a fifth more than the price), and its units sail through them.
• Towns can buy a Canal from their purchase list, and it opens immediately.
• A canal can continue from one settlement's tiles into a neighbor's.
• Ancient, Medieval and Modern Canal each have their own name and icon.
===== comment 2 of 3 =====
Canals 1.1.0, part 2 of 3, fixed:
• Towns were not offered the Canal at all.
• Canals from other ages, and Canals whose tech was not yet researched, appeared in the build lists.
• Founding a settlement next to a neck of land removed that neck as a canal site.
• Tiles holding a resource were offered as sites, and then the order was refused.
• A Garden or Harbor could be built on a finished canal tile.
• A tile touching water on several sides got a channel to every side, like a star. It now gets one channel between its two shores, plus a branch into any separate lake or sea it touches.
• Where two canals met, locks were drawn at the junction. Locks now appear only where a channel meets a cliff shore.
===== comment 3 of 3 =====
Canals 1.1.0, part 3 of 3, good to know:
• While you have a canal, the mod handles autosaving. Your autosave settings still apply.
• Canals in saves from 1.0.0 show as open sea at first. Accept the reload offer when you load to fix them.
• Added the mod to a game after researching the Canal's tech? Buy the Canal with gold from the purchase list.
• Single player only.
• Move ships out of your canals before removing the mod.
