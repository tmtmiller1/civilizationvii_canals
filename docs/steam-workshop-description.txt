[h1]Canals[/h1]
[b]Version 1.1.1. For Civilization VII 1.5.0.[/b]
Cut a ship channel through a narrow neck of land. Build the Canal on a land tile between two shores; when it is finished the tile becomes water, ships sail through it, and it is drawn as a canal for its age, with a harbor, houses and boats along the banks. AI players dig canals and sail through them too.
[h2]Mechanics[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]A Canal for each age[/b]: the Ancient, Medieval and Modern Canal, in the ordinary production list, unlocked by Engineering, Shipbuilding and Industrialization, at 300, 500 and 800 production. A settlement can dig as many canals as it has sites for.
[*][b]Towns too.[/b] A town buys its Canal with gold from its purchase list, like any other building; a bought Canal opens at once.
[*][b]Canal sites only.[/b] The placement screen offers a land tile the city owns, within its build ring, with sea, lake or navigable river on at least two separate sides. A canal can join a river to the sea or two reaches of a river. A tile that holds a resource takes no building and is not offered.
[*][b]Ships go through the same turn.[/b] When the Canal completes the tile becomes water and any ship can path through it at once.
[*][b]Canals last.[/b] A canal is still a channel cut through land after a save and reload.
[*][b]Longer canals.[/b] From the Exploration age a second Canal in a straight line with the first cuts a two-tile passage; in the Modern age runs of up to five tiles may bend and branch. Neighboring canals join into one waterway, and a canal may run on from one city's or town's land into another's.
[*][b]A worked tile with its own yields.[/b] The finished canal is a fishing tile of the city, and the Canal stays on it as a building with its own food and gold: 2 and 2 in Antiquity, 3 and 4 in Exploration, 4 and 6 in Modern.
[*][b]Ships through a canal per turn:[/b] one for an Antiquity canal, two for Exploration, unlimited for Modern.
[*][b]Drawn for its age.[/b] A channel between its shores, one branch into any other body of water it touches, moored boats at the mouths, and an Antiquity harbor and quay, Exploration houses and a wharf, or Modern buildings and a harbor along the banks. Three looks per age.
[/list]
[h2]AI canals[/h2]
AI players build Canals themselves, as they build any building, and only on a canal site: the mod marks, for each AI, every tile of its settlements where a canal saves a ship at least six tiles of sailing round or opens a lake or sea of ten tiles or more, and the game offers the Canal there and nowhere else. The AI decides when to build it, with production or gold. AI canals open and are drawn like yours, and AI units sail through them.
[h2]Settlement centers[/h2]
A city or town center counts as a canal end, so a canal can run from the settlement's own tile out to the sea or a river.
[h2]Cliffs[/h2]
A canal can be cut through a tile with cliffs, and ships cross every edge once it opens. Where the channel meets a shore that was a cliff, it steps down through a lock: a stone chamber with gates, a gatehouse and a water wheel.
[h2]Autosaves[/h2]
While a canal is open, the mod writes the autosave at the start of your turn, on your autosave frequency and keep count. The files carry on the game's own autosave series, so the newest is at the top of the Autosaves tab and is the one Continue loads.
[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Civilization VII 1.5.0.[/b] No base-game files are replaced and no base-game rows change; three buildings and their tech unlocks are added.
[*][b]A save loads with or without Canals enabled.[/b] Without it, a dug canal is a plain land tile of the city, and a ship left in it is stranded: move your ships out of the canals before you remove the mod.
[*][b]Added to a game under way[/b] after the Canal's tech was researched, the game keeps that age's Canal locked; the mod sells it for gold in each settlement's purchase list instead.
[*][b]Saves from Canals 1.0.0[/b] hold their canals as open water and load drawn as open sea. The mod offers to reload at once, which draws them as canals again.
[*][b]Works with [url=https://github.com/tmtmiller1/civilizationvii_national-parks]National Park[/url].[/b] Park land is never offered as a Canal site, whichever of the two mods loads first.
[*][b]Works with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3810140802]Dams[/url].[/b] A Dam cannot be built on an opened canal, and a Canal is never offered on a tile that holds a Dam.
[*][b]Single-player games only.[/b] In a multiplayer game no Canal can be built, and the mod says so when the game starts.
[*][b]English only.[/b]
[/list]
