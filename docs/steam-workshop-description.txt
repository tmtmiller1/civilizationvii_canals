[h1]Canals[/h1]
[b]Version 1.0.0, the first release. For Civilization VII 1.5.0.[/b]
Cut a ship channel through a narrow neck of land. Build the Canal on a land tile between two shores; when it is finished the tile becomes water, ships sail through it, and it is drawn as a canal for its age, with a harbour, houses and boats along the banks.
[h2]Mechanics[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Compatible with 1.5.0.[/b]
[*][b]A Canal for each age[/b], in the ordinary production list, unlocked by Engineering, Shipbuilding and Industrialization, at 300, 500 and 800 production. A city can dig as many canals as it has sites for.
[*][b]Canal sites only.[/b] The placement screen offers a land tile the city owns, within its build ring, with sea, lake or navigable river on at least two separate sides. A canal can join a river to the sea or two reaches of a river.
[*][b]Ships go through the same turn.[/b] When the Canal completes the tile becomes water and any ship can path through it at once. The change survives a save and reload.
[*][b]Longer canals.[/b] From the Exploration age a second Canal in a straight line with the first cuts a two-tile passage; in the Modern age runs of up to five tiles that may bend and branch. Each opening redraws its neighbours so the channels meet.
[*][b]A worked tile with its own yields.[/b] The finished canal is a fishing tile of the city, and the Canal stays on it as a building with its own food and gold: 2 and 2 in Antiquity, 3 and 4 in Exploration, 4 and 6 in Modern.
[*][b]Ships through a canal per turn:[/b] one for an Antiquity canal, two for Exploration, unlimited for Modern.
[*][b]Drawn for its age.[/b] A channel toward each shore, moored boats at the mouths, and an Antiquity harbour and quay, Exploration houses and a wharf, or Modern buildings and a harbour along the banks. Three looks per age.
[/list]
[h2]Settlement centres[/h2]
A city or town centre counts as a canal end, so a canal can run from the settlement's own tile out to the sea or a river.
[h2]Cliffs[/h2]
A canal can be cut through a tile with cliffs. Ships cross every edge once it opens. The cliff rock faces stay drawn until the game is next loaded, so at a cliff shore the canal is drawn stepping down through a lock: a stone chamber with gates, a gatehouse, a water wheel and water spilling over the cliff foot. Cosmetic only.
[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]No base-game files are replaced[/b] and no base-game rows change. The mod adds three buildings and their tech unlocks.
[*][b]A save loads with the mod on or off.[/b] A canal already dug is ordinary map state: a water tile worked by the city.
[*][b]A game started before the mod was enabled[/b] gets the Canal when its unlocking tech is researched.
[*][b]Single player.[/b] In a network game the Canal is not offered.
[/list]
[h2]Languages[/h2]
English only for now. The mod's text is a handful of strings, so a translation is a small job: ask for a language and I will add it.
